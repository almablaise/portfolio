## Concept

alba was conceived through the cycle of the sun, at the transition from day to night. It is a sangria brand that adds a touch of warmth and conviviality through two bottles to be enjoyed at different times of the evening — giving sangria a warm image reminiscent of its Latin origins, with a touch of modernity and delicacy.

![Moodboard](/images/projects/alba/moodboard.jpg)

## Campaign

Plants are present to highlight the warm, evasive side of sangria, represented in engravings in the brand’s colours. The campaign invites people to photograph themselves at sunrise or sunset with the corresponding alba bottle and share it on Instagram.
