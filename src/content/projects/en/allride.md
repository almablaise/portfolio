## Context

AllRide is the result of a year-long research project conducted in Spanish during my Master’s at BAU Barcelona. It explores how people facing commuting challenges could share long-term journeys instead of one-off rides — and how a platform could foster teamwork and conviviality on everyday trips.

![Project objectives](/images/projects/allride/10.jpg)

## Discovery

I followed the Double Diamond method. The discovery phase combined a first definition of the problem, objectives and changes, surveys and around twenty user interviews, then post-it workshops to structure the insights.

![Discovery phase](/images/projects/allride/02.jpg)

## The concept

AllRide is an application that simplifies the discovery of users who share the same commuting routine. Unlike other ridesharing apps, it focuses on establishing long-term carpooling connections for routine journeys — with a warm, trustworthy, friendly and simple voice.
